LUCID RESEARCH

Every CS conference deadline,
in one place.

Free, refreshed daily, across nine research areas.

Computer science publishes at conferences, and each one opens its submission window just once a year. Miss it and the paper waits months for the next venue. The dates are public, but they are scattered across dozens of separate calls for papers, and the trackers that do exist are split by field — so anyone working across areas has no single place to look. This is that place: 185+ venues pulled together from the open trackers and refreshed every day.
